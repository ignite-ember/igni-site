igni — AI agents that run inside your network

igni is the AI platform regulated organisations can actually run: agents for every team and models trained on your own data, while no AI vendor sees or holds your code, data or models, Ignite Ember included. The base is free under Apache-2.0. Paid modules start at €8k a year, and each can be evaluated free for 30 days, or 60 with your integrator.

Inside your network

igni runs on GPUs you own, or dedicated GPUs you rent, and starts on one GPU. It runs air-gapped with no outbound connections of its own, and Ignite Ember has no route into your network. Models built from your data have no licence key and no expiry. One installation serves every person and every app.

The igni base, free under Apache-2.0

Agents and orchestration, igni's models, MCP plugins into your systems, OIDC sign-in, SCIM provisioning, roles and an audit trail, and the desktop app and CLI. SAML sign-in is coming next.

Modules, each an annual licence

  • CodeIndex — answers and reviews from a map of your code. €12k a year for up to 50 developers.
  • Artifacts — a document graph that rebuilds when a source changes. €8k a year for up to 50 people; readers are free.
  • Fleet — a cloud agent in an isolated VM for every person and app. €10k a year for up to 50 people, with as many apps; local agents are free.
  • Factory — domain models trained on your data, and yours to keep. €40k a year for any number of models. Autopilot, on top, from €10k a year for up to 50 people.

One installation, four departments

Start with one engineering team, where igni reviews every pull and merge request. Add risk and compliance, where every document built on a source is flagged when that source changes; operations, with a cloud agent for every person and every app on your servers; and legal, with a contract-review model trained on your legal team's material. The data, the agents and the results stay on your servers.

Security and continuity

Each release ships as a signed bundle with a software bill of materials, which you verify and apply on your schedule. Before the first workshop, your security team receives a pack: the egress test, the SBOM, a network diagram, an exit-test runbook and a compliance self-assessment. No DPA is needed, because Ignite Ember never processes your data. igni hosts nothing, so like PostgreSQL there is no service to certify: it runs inside your own certified estate, and it is assessed as a product under the EU Cyber Resilience Act. If you stop paying, a module runs for 90 days with warnings, then becomes read-only; the base and your models keep running. Each paid module's source goes into verified escrow, set up with your first order.

Your own apps inside igni

Everyone works in the igni desktop app, and developers can also use a CLI. Your developers or your integrator can build apps into it with the igni SDKs, adding pages, tools, agents, commands and settings. Each app runs on your servers, and an administrator approves it before anyone receives it.

Pricing

A flat annual fee per module, set by how many people use it, up to an unlimited tier above 1,000. People who only read a module's output are not counted, and neither are agents or tokens. Nothing is metered. List prices in EUR per year, excluding VAT; your integrator quotes the final price.

How you buy

Try it free: the Apache-2.0 base and every paid module on evaluation, with nothing to sign. Pilot it in production for up to 90 days with no licence fee, against success criteria you agree in writing; the order lapses if they are not met. The annual licence starts only when the criteria are met.

For integrators

Install igni, take it through your client's accreditation, connect identity and CI, and provide first- and second-line support. Ignite Ember supplies the software, the models and signed update bundles, and handles third-line support.
